<span class="planned">Planned</span>

Turn finished work into published content, in your voice. The bottleneck is the publishing loop, not the ideas, so this domain exists to close it.

## Planned skills
- `luck-surface` — turn a conversation or doc into a thread or post: draft, review gate, publish.
- `marayoo-style` — visual content for a show: covers, thumbnails, reels.
- `nano-banana` — image generation for mockups, logos, concept art.
- `humanize` — strip the tells of AI writing.
- a `video` pipeline — script to shotlist to render.

See the [Architecture](../../architecture/) for how domains compose.
