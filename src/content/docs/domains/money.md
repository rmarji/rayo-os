<span class="planned">Planned</span>

Spend, pricing, and capital decisions grounded in real numbers, not vibes.

## Planned skills
- `monarch` — query accounts, transactions, and cashflow; detect spending leaks.
- `fate-money` — the FATE go/no-go applied to a spend or investment, priced at opportunity cost.
- `pricing` — value-based pricing and proposal structure: rate cards, retainers, three-option offers.
