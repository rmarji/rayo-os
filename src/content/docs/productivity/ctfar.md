> Stuck or reacting? Separate the facts from the story you tell about them.

## When to use

Emotionally stuck, ruminating, avoiding, or processing a decision or relationship. Not a substitute for making the call once you are unstuck.

## How it works

- **C — Circumstance**: camera-only facts. No adjectives, no interpretation.
- **T — Thought**: the story about the C. The hook lives here.
- **F — Feeling**: what the T produces.
- **A — Action**: what the F drives (including inaction).
- **R — Result**: what the A creates, usually proving the T.

Find the T driving the loop, then test it.

## Try it

`ctfar I keep putting off a hard conversation` →

- C: it is unscheduled. T: "if I raise it, I look needy." F: anxious. A: avoid. R: nothing changes. The hook is the T.

## Pitfalls

- Writing interpretation into C. Camera-only, or the whole thing tilts.
- Using the analysis to avoid the feeling. If it is becoming another matrix to dodge an emotion, name that and stay in F.

## Leads to

- unstuck → [route](../route/) (now make the call)
- a pattern worth keeping → log it
