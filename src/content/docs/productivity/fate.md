> Go or no-go on an opportunity, in four numbers.

## When to use

A new opportunity is on the table and you need a fast, honest go/no-go: a deal, a project, a feature, a "should I build this." Not for choosing among options already committed to (that is [mcdm](../mcdm/)).

## How it works

Score each 1-3 (max 12):

- **Fit** — skills, identity, your outcomes; kills shiny objects.
- **Asymmetry** — capped loss plus uncapped gain (Taleb / Naval).
- **Timing** — why now, not last year, not next year.
- **Effort** — true cost in hours, attention, capital at your hourly opportunity cost.

The four force the tradeoff that "it sounds cool" hides.

## Try it

`fate a new feature idea` →

- Fit 3 · Asymmetry 2 · Timing 3 · Effort 2 = **10/12 → pursue**
- one line of why per dimension; the lowest score is what to watch.

Verdict: **>=9 pursue · 6-8 park** (set a revisit trigger) **· <=5 kill**. Any dimension at 1 = flag for override, do not auto-kill.

## Pitfalls

- Scoring to justify a call you already made. Name the criteria before scoring.
- Using a high FATE to skip the Ship Gate. A "pursue" still does not let you start until one thing is shipped.
- Treating "park" as "no" — park means revisit on a trigger, not bury.

## Leads to

- pursue → [ship-gate](../ship-gate/) (am I allowed to start yet?)
- close call → [mcdm](../mcdm/) (weigh against alternatives)
- emotional charge on the call → [ctfar](../ctfar/)
