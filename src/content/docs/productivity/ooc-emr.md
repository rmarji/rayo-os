> Big, values-level calls a matrix can't hold. Generate, weigh, then blend.

## When to use

Career moves, relationships, where to live — decisions too deep for a plain scorecard. Not for a quick option-ranking (that is [mcdm](../mcdm/)).

## How it works

- **Outcomes** — what do you actually want here (the real goal under the decision)?
- **Options** — generate more than the obvious two.
- **Consequences** — for each option, upside and downside weighted by probability.
- **Evaluate** — against the outcomes.
- **Mitigate** — the move most people skip: blend options to keep the upside and shave the downside. It is rarely pure A vs B.
- **Resolve** — decide and commit.

## Try it

`ooc-emr where to base next year` →

- the real outcome named, three or more options, a Mitigate blend (e.g. "one base plus two months elsewhere"), a resolution.

## Pitfalls

- Skipping Mitigate and forcing a binary. The blend is usually the answer.
- Deciding before naming the real Outcome (you will optimize the wrong thing).
