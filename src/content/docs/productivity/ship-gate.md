> Should-I is FATE. How-many is your WIP limit. This is: am I allowed to start yet?

## When to use

About to start something new — a project, a build, a thread — or the tell "I'll just build a tool/system for X." Fires at the moment of admission, not mid-build.

## How it works

**The rule:** nothing new starts until one thing is told. *Told* means value reached a human who isn't you (published, sent, demoed). "Saved", "it compiles", "draft done" do not count.

1. Name the candidate.
2. Check the gate: any active thread with an untold output? If yes, gate CLOSED — the candidate parks.
3. Open the gate: ship one untold thing; state who received it and where.
4. Log it (closes Luck = Doing x Telling).
5. Admit, subject to your go/no-go and WIP limit.

## Try it

`ship-gate a new side project` →

- gate CLOSED: last week's draft is still unpublished → park the project until you ship it.

## Pitfalls

- Counting "built the decider" as shipping. A new framework, app, or matrix is deciding, not shipping.
- Gaming "told" with a throwaway nobody was meant to receive.

- told item → log it
